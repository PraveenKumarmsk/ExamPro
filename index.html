Our story

Built by aspirants, for aspirants.

ExamPro started because we saw too many aspirants paying for content that didn't help, or worse — didn't exist. We wanted a platform that showed real progress, real analytics, and real questions, without inflated numbers or fake testimonials.

Every mock test, note, and question is reviewed before it goes live. We're building ExamPro in public — adding tests and materials as they're verified, and keeping the free tier genuinely useful.

  • ✓ Honest content — no fake stats, no invented leaderboards
  • ✓ Bilingual — English & Hindi with clear labelling
  • ✓ Free tier that actually works, forever
  • ✓ Focused on what moves your score

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is ExamPro free to use?

Yes — daily quizzes, selected mock tests, and basic analytics are free forever. Premium plans unlock the full test series and advanced analytics.

Which exams are covered?

SSC, Railway, BPSC & Bihar exams, Banking (IBPS/SBI), UPSC, State PCS, and Police & Defence exams. New exams are added regularly.

Can I create my own test?

Yes. Use the Custom Test Builder to select exam, subject, chapter, topic, difficulty, question count, and duration.

Are previous year papers official?

Only where licensing permits. Demo content is clearly labelled and not represented as official exam material.

Do you support Hindi?

Bilingual support is being rolled out. Where a Hindi translation isn't available, the question clearly indicates it rather than showing incorrect translations.

Is my data safe?

We use secure authentication, encrypted connections, and never share personal information publicly.
