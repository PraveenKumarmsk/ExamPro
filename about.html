Our story

ExamPro started because we saw too many aspirants paying for content that didn't help, or worse, didn't exist. We wanted a platform that showed real progress, real analytics, and real questions — without inflated numbers. We're building this in public, adding tests and materials as they're verified, and keeping the free tier genuinely useful.
