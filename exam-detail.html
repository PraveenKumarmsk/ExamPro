About this exam

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) exam recruits graduates for Group B and Group C posts across various ministries, departments, and organisations of the Government of India. It is conducted in multiple tiers, with Tier 1 being a computer-based test covering General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Comprehension.
